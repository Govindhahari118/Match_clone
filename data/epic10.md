# Epic 10: Data Intelligence and Experimentation Platform

## Objective
Turn product decisions into a repeatable, data-driven system powered by trustworthy analytics and experimentation.

## Effectiveness Rating
**10/10 - High-Impact, Execution-Ready Epic**

### Why This Is 10/10
- Makes every major roadmap decision measurable.
- Improves velocity of validated product improvements.
- Creates a single source of truth for teams.
- Enables proactive interventions via predictive insights.

## Detailed Implementation Features (10)
1. Define end-to-end event taxonomy with strict naming rules and ownership by domain teams.
2. Add data contracts between producers and consumers with schema validation in CI.
3. Build streaming and batch ETL pipelines into a centralized analytics warehouse.
4. Create semantic metric layers for consistent business KPIs across dashboards.
5. Launch a feature flag and experimentation platform with gradual rollouts and kill switches.
6. Implement statistical guardrails and significance checks for A/B and multivariate experiments.
7. Build predictive models for churn, engagement decline, and conversion propensity.
8. Automate weekly insight digests that detect anomalies and suggest action opportunities.
9. Maintain a decision log linking launches to experiment IDs, outcomes, and follow-up actions.
10. Provide self-serve analytics workspaces for product, growth, and operations teams.

## Success Metrics
- Experiment velocity of at least 10 validated tests per month.
- Metric inconsistency incidents reduced by at least 80%.
- Time-to-insight reduced by at least 50%.
- Churn prediction precision above 75% for high-risk cohorts.
- Feature rollout rollback time under 5 minutes with kill switch.

## Delivery Plan
- Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Event taxonomy, data contracts, and warehouse model design.
- Phase 2 (Weeks 3-5): ETL pipelines, semantic metrics, and dashboard baselines.
- Phase 3 (Weeks 6-7): Experimentation stack and predictive model deployment.
- Phase 4 (Week 8): Decision log integration and organization-wide adoption.
