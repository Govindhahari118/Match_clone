# Epic 5: Onboarding and Profile Completion Optimization

## Objective
Maximize activation and profile quality by guiding users through a fast, intelligent, and trust-building onboarding flow.

## Effectiveness Rating
**10/10 - High-Impact, Execution-Ready Epic**

### Why This Is 10/10
- Improves first impression and early retention.
- Directly increases profile quality, which improves matching accuracy.
- Reduces friction with adaptive form logic.
- Includes measurable funnel and quality outcomes.

## Detailed Implementation Features (10)
1. Redesign onboarding into adaptive steps that prioritize essential fields first and defer optional details.
2. Implement progressive disclosure rules so users only see context-relevant fields.
3. Add a profile completeness engine with weighted scoring and actionable completion prompts.
4. Deploy photo quality assistant checks for blur, face framing, and lighting before upload acceptance.
5. Enable interrupted-flow resume with cross-device state persistence.
6. Build reminder journeys through push, email, and in-app nudges based on drop-off behavior.
7. Integrate verification progress and privacy setup directly into onboarding.
8. Add guided preference setup with examples to improve downstream match quality.
9. Localize onboarding content and culturally sensitive fields across key regions.
10. Run onboarding experiments (copy, step order, field grouping) with funnel analytics and cohort reporting.

## Success Metrics
- Onboarding completion rate above 75%.
- Average time-to-first-match under 24 hours.
- Complete-profile rate (80%+) increased by at least 35%.
- Early 7-day retention improved by at least 20%.
- Photo approval first-pass rate above 85%.

## Delivery Plan
- Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): New onboarding architecture and completion scoring.
- Phase 2 (Weeks 3-5): Photo assistant, resume flow, and reminder engine.
- Phase 3 (Weeks 6-7): Localization, preference guidance, and verification integration.
- Phase 4 (Week 8): Experiment optimization and full-scale release.
