# Epic 8: Admin and Operations Automation Suite

## Objective
Scale trust and support operations with faster decisions, automation, and consistent enforcement quality.

## Effectiveness Rating
**10/10 - High-Impact, Execution-Ready Epic**

### Why This Is 10/10
- Reduces operational bottlenecks as user base grows.
- Improves moderation consistency and support quality.
- Converts manual workflows into reliable automation.
- Provides clear accountability with auditable actions.

## Detailed Implementation Features (10)
1. Build a unified admin workspace combining user risk, reports, verification state, and payment status.
2. Implement full case lifecycle management with timeline views, notes, ownership, and status transitions.
3. Add SLA-based queues with severity routing and automatic escalation when response deadlines are missed.
4. Create a policy rule engine for auto-actions (auto-hold, auto-close duplicates, auto-escalate severe abuse).
5. Enforce role-based access controls and approval gates for high-risk administrative actions.
6. Add safe bulk operations for repetitive tasks with preview, rollback, and audit support.
7. Integrate support tooling for shared context between trust and customer support teams.
8. Build operations analytics for queue health, resolution times, agent productivity, and policy outcomes.
9. Attach contextual runbooks and standard operating procedures directly inside admin workflows.
10. Introduce quality assurance sampling to review moderator decisions and reduce policy drift.

## Success Metrics
- Moderation backlog reduced by at least 60%.
- Mean time to resolve high-priority cases under 30 minutes.
- Policy-consistent decision rate above 95%.
- Manual repetitive operations reduced by at least 50%.
- Agent throughput increased by at least 25%.

## Delivery Plan
- Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Unified admin data model and queue foundations.
- Phase 2 (Weeks 3-5): Case workflows, RBAC, and policy rule automation.
- Phase 3 (Weeks 6-7): Bulk actions, support integration, and QA sampling.
- Phase 4 (Week 8): KPI tuning, operational playbooks, and final rollout.
