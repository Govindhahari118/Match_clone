MatrimonyConnect Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when creating and managing your profile:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender
  • Profile Information: Photos, bio, education, profession, religion, caste, mother tongue, family details, hobbies, and partner preferences
  • Astrological Data: Rasi (zodiac sign) and Nakshatra (birth star) for compatibility matching
  • Location Data: City, state, country of residence (entered manually, not GPS-tracked)
  • Communication Data: Messages exchanged within the app (encrypted end-to-end)
  • Payment Information: Payment details are processed by Razorpay; we do not store credit card numbers
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens for notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Display your profile to other users for matchmaking purposes
  • Calculate compatibility scores based on questionnaire responses and astrological data
  • Send notifications about matches, interests, and messages
  • Process premium subscription payments
  • Detect and prevent fake profiles and fraudulent activity
  • Improve app functionality through anonymized analytics

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With other registered users as part of the matchmaking service (your profile is visible to potential matches)
  • With Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, analytics, and crash reporting
  • With Razorpay for payment processing
  • When required by law or to protect safety

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Profile data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore with encryption at rest
  • Chat messages are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before transmission
  • Passwords are hashed using BCrypt (never stored in plain text)
  • Photos are stored in Firebase Storage with access controls
  • We use biometric authentication (optional) for additional security

5. Your Rights

  • Access: View all data associated with your account in the app
  • Correction: Edit your profile information at any time
  • Deletion: Delete your account permanently from Settings (removes all data from our servers)
  • Portability: Export your profile data from Settings
  • Blocking: Block any user to prevent them from seeing your profile
  • Photo Privacy: Control who can see your photos (match-only or public)

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion:

  • Profile data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Chat messages with other users are anonymized
  • Payment records are retained for 7 years as required by law
  • Sessions expire automatically after 30 days of inactivity

7. Cookies & Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage patterns. This includes:

  • Screen views and navigation patterns
  • Feature usage statistics
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

No third-party advertising trackers are used.

8. Children's Privacy

MatrimonyConnect is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a user is under 18, their account will be terminated immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Users will be notified of significant changes through in-app notifications.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries:
