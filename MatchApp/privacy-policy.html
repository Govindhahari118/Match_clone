Matree Privacy Policy - Release Candidate Draft

Implementation baseline: September 29, 2026.

Release notice: This repository copy describes the implemented data flows for engineering review. It must receive final legal approval and be published at the production HTTPS policy URL before public launch.

1. Data Matree processes

  • Account and authentication: Firebase Authentication identifiers, email and/or phone information used by the authentication methods you choose.
  • Profile and matchmaking: information you provide for your profile, including demographic, education, profession, family, lifestyle, religion/community, partner-preference and optional astrology information.
  • Profile media: photos and, only when separately enabled, video or other media. Profile photos are protected in Firebase Storage and require backend moderation approval before peer publication.
  • Communication: messages and authorized chat media exchanged after a mutual match. Matree does not market this transport as end-to-end encrypted; device-local cached message bodies are protected using Android Keystore-backed encryption.
  • Verification: verification status and, when you explicitly use an enabled verification flow, protected identity evidence. Raw KYC artifacts are not readable by ordinary member clients.
  • Location: city/state/profile location you enter and, only when you opt into Nearby, foreground device location. Nearby can use approximate or precise foreground location; exact coordinates are stored in a server-only record and are never returned to other members.
  • Safety and support: blocks, reports, support requests, consent records, moderation state and private risk signals used to operate the service and investigate abuse.
  • Purchases: Google Play purchase and entitlement records required to verify paid membership or other supported digital purchases. Matree does not store payment-card numbers.
  • Device and diagnostics: per-installation FCM registration, app version, product telemetry, Firebase Analytics events and Crashlytics diagnostics where those services are enabled in the production environment.

2. Why Matree uses this data

  • Authenticate accounts and protect account lifecycle actions.
  • Provide profiles, search, recommendations, preferences, interests, matches and authorized communication.
  • Provide optional verification, Nearby and paid features only when those capabilities are enabled and their release gates pass.
  • Detect abuse signals, enforce blocks and moderation decisions, operate support, and protect service integrity.
  • Deliver transactional and user-configured notifications.
  • Verify Google Play purchases and reconcile entitlement state.
  • Measure reliability and diagnose crashes without intentionally placing private message content, KYC documents or exact location coordinates into analytics events.

3. Visibility and sharing

  • Profile fields intended for matchmaking may be visible to authenticated members who are eligible to view your profile under current privacy, block, lifecycle and visibility rules.
  • Contact details are kept outside the public discovery profile and are disclosed only through the server-controlled contact flow where the applicable relationship and entitlement rules pass.
  • Profile media access is checked against current account, block, privacy and publication state. Uploading an object alone does not make it public.
  • Exact Nearby coordinates are not shared with other members; Nearby returns only authorized profile identity and computed distance information.
  • Service providers such as Google/Firebase and Google Play process information needed to provide their configured services under their own terms and data-processing obligations.
  • Matree does not sell personal data to advertisers.

4. Controls, consent and account lifecycle

  • You can edit supported profile fields and partner preferences, manage notification and privacy settings, block members, pause matrimony discovery, and stop Nearby sharing.
  • Sensitive optional processing uses explicit consent records where implemented. Withdrawing a consent disables the associated processing path and triggers the relevant cleanup where the feature contract requires it.
  • Settings provides an authenticated account-data export flow and a permanent account-deletion flow.
  • Account deletion immediately suppresses the public profile and runs resumable backend cleanup. Some records may be retained only when an applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention or financial obligation requires retention; the final production retention schedule must be approved by legal counsel.

5. Security

  • Release builds disable cleartext network traffic and use Firebase App Check with Play Integrity support.
  • Server-owned fields such as verification, membership, moderation, risk and entitlement state cannot be self-granted by the Android client.
  • Protected media uses Firebase Storage authorization and canonical object paths rather than treating long-lived download URLs as authorization.
  • Operations actions are role-scoped and sensitive reviews create audit evidence.
  • No security mechanism eliminates all risk. Members should use in-app reporting and blocking for suspicious behavior and contact appropriate emergency services for immediate danger.

6. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the data category, account state, enabled providers and applicable legal obligations. The application contains explicit cleanup for account profile data, protected media, exact Nearby location, notification/device state, consent-related feature data and other account-scoped records. The public release policy must state the legally approved production retention periods; this engineering copy intentionally does not invent fixed periods.

7. Age requirement

Matree is intended for adults aged 18 and above. Accounts that do not meet the eligibility rules may be restricted or removed.

8. Questions and rights requests

Use the authenticated Help & Support flow in the app for privacy, access, correction, export, deletion, consent or grievance requests. Production contact details and jurisdiction-specific rights language must be confirmed by the release owner and legal reviewer before publication.
