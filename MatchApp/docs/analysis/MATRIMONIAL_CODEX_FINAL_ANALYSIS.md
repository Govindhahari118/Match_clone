## 4. UI/UX PATTERNS & BEST PRACTICES

### 4.1 Trust & Safety Patterns
1. **Verification Badges** — Blue tick (Shaadi), Phone verified (BharatMatrimony), Triple verified (Absolute)
2. **Trust Indicators** — Years in service, marriages count, customer count, awards, media mentions
3. **Safety Center** — Dedicated page with tips, reporting, blocking guidelines
4. **Watermarked Photos** — Vivaah prevents photo misuse
5. **Screenshot Prevention** — TrulyMadly blocks screenshots
6. **No Phone Sharing** — SecondSutra never reveals phone numbers
7. **Accept/Decline Gate** — Contact only after mutual acceptance

### 4.2 Search & Discovery Patterns
1. **Quick Search** — Minimal filters (age, religion) on homepage
2. **Advanced Search** — Full filter set (15+ parameters) behind "Advanced" button
3. **Keyword Search** — MatchFinder, Vivaah support free-text search
4. **Browse Tabs** — Community/Religion/City/State/Profession/Mother Tongue tabs
5. **Daily Recommendations** — AI-powered daily match suggestions
6. **Nearby Matches** — Location-based proximity search
7. **Recently Joined** — New profiles section
8. **Profile ID Search** — Direct lookup by ID number
9. **Saved Searches** — Store and re-run search criteria

### 4.3 Communication Patterns
1. **Express Interest** — One-tap interest sending (lightweight first contact)
2. **Message Templates** — Pre-written message options
3. **Premium Messaging** — Full chat behind paywall
4. **24-Hour Chat Window** — Time-limited chat (SecondSutra)
5. **Video Call** — In-app video calling (premium feature)
6. **Voice Messages** — Audio message recording and playback
7. **Virtual Family Meet** — Video call specifically for family introductions (M4Marry)
8. **Read Receipts** — Message seen indicators

### 4.4 Engagement Patterns
1. **Success Stories** — Carousel of real couple stories
2. **Blog/Guides** — Educational content (relationship advice, wedding planning)
3. **FAQ Accordion** — Expandable Q&A sections
4. **TV Commercials** — Embedded video ads
5. **Celebrity Endorsements** — Brand ambassador integration
6. **Web Stories** — Instagram-like visual stories (MatrimonialsIndia)
7. **Video Profiles/Shorts** — Video introductions (M4Marry)
8. **App Download CTA** — QR code + store badges
9. **Testimonials with Photos** — Real user reviews with photos and ratings

### 4.5 Monetization Patterns
1. **Tiered Subscriptions** — Free/Silver/Gold/Platinum/Diamond
2. **Duration-Based** — 3/6/12 months or "Till U Marry"
3. **Contact Credits** — Limited number of profile contacts per plan
4. **Profile Boost** — Paid visibility enhancement
5. **Featured Profile** — Highlighted in search results
6. **Assisted Service** — Premium RM-based matchmaking
7. **Money-Back Guarantee** — 30-day (Shaadi) or 18-month (LoveVivah)
8. **Credit-Based Meets** — Per-meeting pricing (SecondSutra ₹500/meet)
9. **Franchise Model** — Associate/franchise opportunities (AbsoluteMatrimony)
